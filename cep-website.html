Public Accountability
ASK CEP ANYTHING
How will CEP stop corruption?
Every rupee spent by CEP will be publicly logged on this website and the app. We use device-verified transaction records, open ledgers, and mandatory RTI compliance. No cash dealings — everything is traceable.
How will jobs be created?
Through skill-match employment boards, local manufacturing incentives, and direct youth startup funding. We reject "promise-based" schemes — our jobs plan links government contracts directly to measurable employment output.
Why minimum 12th pass for candidates?
A basic minimum educational standard ensures that people making policy have at least a foundational level of literacy and reasoning skills. It's not about elitism — it's about accountability and competence.
Is CEP a registered political party?
CEP is currently a registered youth political community actively working through the Election Commission process. Our legal team is finalizing formal registration. We operate with full transparency even as an unofficial entity.
